01 / Research

Research interests

My research is primarily in model theory and categorical logic, with a particular interest in topos-theoretic methods.

Current research

My PhD project concerns categorical approaches to model theory and stability theory.

  1. 01

    Model theory

    The study of mathematical structures through the logical theories they satisfy, with a particular interest in classification questions and stability-theoretic methods.

  2. 02

    Categorical logic

    Categorical semantics and the relationship between logical theories, their models and interpretations, especially where logical structure is captured by categorical constructions.

  3. 03

    Topos theory

    Topos-theoretic methods in logic and model theory, and the ways in which geometric and categorical structures offer new perspectives on mathematical theories.
